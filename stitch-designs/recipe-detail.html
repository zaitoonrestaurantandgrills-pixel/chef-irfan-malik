Signature Dish

Chicken Karahi

A vibrant, deeply savory stir-fried chicken curry from the Indian subcontinent, distinguished by its rich tomato base and fiery green chilies.

Cuisine Pakistani
Difficulty Intermediate
Prep Time 15m
Cook Time 30m
Servings 4

About This Recipe

The true art of a karahi lies in the 'bhunai'—the rigorous stir-frying of the meat in its own juices and spices over high heat. This recipe eschews onions, relying entirely on ripe, jammy tomatoes for its luscious gravy, a hallmark of authentic roadside dhabas. The resulting dish is intensely flavorful, clinging perfectly to fresh naan.

Ingredients Preview

  • 1 kg Bone-in Chicken, cut into small pieces
  • 6 large Roma Tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 1/2 cup Ghee or Neutral Oil
  • 4-5 Thai Green Chilies, slit
